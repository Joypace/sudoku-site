Sudoku Social Publisher: terms of use

Updated November 12, 2026.

Sudoku Social Publisher is a private desktop utility for the authorized operator of Sudoku Let's Try to upload gameplay videos to the operator's social accounts. It is not a public account-management service and does not offer public registration or subscriptions.

Authorized use

Connect only an account you own or are authorized to manage. Upload only content you have permission to share, including its images, video and music. Your use must comply with the destination platform's terms and content policies.

TikTok drafts

The TikTok integration is being configured. When enabled, it will request permission to upload selected videos as drafts. Uploading a draft does not automatically publish it. Review and complete the post in TikTok. TikTok controls processing, availability, moderation and any posting limits.

Account security and availability

Keep account credentials and authorization tokens private. Revoke the connection through the destination platform when it is no longer needed. The utility may stop working when permissions expire or a provider changes its API. Inspect the saved upload status before repeating an interrupted operation to avoid duplicate uploads.

Privacy and contact

See the publisher privacy notice. Questions about this utility can be sent to sudoku@joypace.com.
