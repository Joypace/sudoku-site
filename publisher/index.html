Sudoku Let's Try

Sudoku Social Publisher

A private desktop utility for the authorized operator of Sudoku Let's Try to transfer selected gameplay videos to the operator's social accounts.

The TikTok integration is being configured. When enabled, it will upload videos as drafts for the account owner to review and finish posting in TikTok. This utility does not offer public signup or automatic TikTok publication.
