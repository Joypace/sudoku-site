Sudoku Social Publisher: privacy notice

Updated November 12, 2026.

This notice describes the private desktop publishing utility used by the authorized operator of Sudoku Let's Try. It is separate from the game and website privacy policy.

The private TikTok connection is being tested. This notice covers account authorization and any uploads the operator initiates.

Account connection

When the operator authorizes the TikTok connection, it requests basic profile access to identify the authorized account and the video.upload permission to send selected videos as drafts. Authorization takes place on TikTok's website. The utility exchanges the resulting authorization code for tokens and stores credentials locally with access limited to the operating-system account running it.

Video transfer and records

When the operator initiates an upload, selected video files are sent directly to TikTok's official API. TikTok processes them under its own terms and privacy policy. The utility keeps local records such as file hashes, upload identifiers and processing status so an interrupted operation can be inspected before retrying. Credentials are excluded from ordinary progress output and support reports.

Retention and choices

Local source videos, credentials and upload records remain until the operator removes them. Access can be revoked in TikTok's connected-app settings. Drafts or posts already transferred to TikTok must be managed or deleted in TikTok; deleting local files does not remove those copies.

Website and support

This utility does not sell personal information or add advertising analytics to uploads. Opening these notice pages sends ordinary web-request information to the website and its hosting provider, as explained in the website privacy policy. If the operator chooses to send a support report, only the information included in that report is shared with its recipient.

For questions, contact sudoku@joypace.com. See also the terms of use.
