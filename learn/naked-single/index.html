Naked Single: one candidate remains.

Sometimes the next move is already in one cell: every digit but one has been ruled out.

The idea

Look at a cell’s row, column and 3×3 box. Eliminate digits already present. If only one candidate remains, put it in the cell. A Naked Single can also appear after other sound note eliminations; these examples need only the starting clues.

These are forced-move examples, so they correctly start at Definite. The native cell highlight emphasizes the sole candidate. A double tap at the cell center fills it in Chill Mode. Each play link opens exactly the starting clues shown here; your App’s display settings may differ. r2c1 means row 2, column 1; boxes are numbered left to right, top to bottom.

Only 4 remains at r5c7

  • 2 is excluded by the given 2 at r6c9, in the same box.
  • 3 is excluded by the given 3 at r3c7, in the same column.
  • 5 is excluded by the given 5 at r4c7, in the same column.
  • 6 is excluded by the given 6 at r6c8, in the same box.
  • 7 is excluded by the given 7 at r6c7, in the same column.

Fill r5c7 with 4

All eight other digits are excluded by starting clues. The only candidate left is 4; no guess is needed.
