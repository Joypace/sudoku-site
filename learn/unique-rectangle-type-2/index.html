Unique Rectangle Type 2: one of the two roofs needs the extra digit.

One of the two roofs needs the extra digit.

The idea

In a puzzle known to have one solution, find four originally empty corners in exactly two rows, two columns and two boxes. Two corners contain only X/Y. The other two—the roofs—share a row or column and both contain exactly X/Y/Z. At least one roof must be Z. Remove Z from every other cell that sees both roofs.

Uniqueness is a required premise. Without either roof taking Z, any completion would have an exchangeable X/Y rectangle and therefore a second completion. This conditional argument does not show two solutions of the original unique puzzle. Every example starts at Dilemma with zero forced singles. Its native highlight shows the complete named premises. Each play link opens exactly the starting clues shown here; display settings may differ. r2c1 means row 2, column 1; boxes run left to right, top to bottom.

Example 1 of 3

Unique Rectangle Type 2: example 1

The four originally empty corners are r8c3, r8c8, r9c3, r9c8. They occupy rows 8/9, columns 3/8, and exactly boxes 7/9.

  • The floors, r9c3, r9c8, have only 4/9.
  • The roofs, r8c3, r8c8, each have exactly 4/9/6 and share a row or column.

If neither roof were 6, the four corners would form an alternating 4/9 rectangle. Swapping those two digits in any completion preserves every given and every row, column and box, creating a second completion. The verified unique puzzle forbids that. Therefore at least one roof is 6.

Copy the original twice to explore those two possibilities. Together they cover the required roof-extra disjunction. Because the roofs share a row or column, they cannot both contain the extra digit.

  1. Try 6 at r8c3. This remains a valid partial board.
  2. Try 6 at r8c8. This remains a valid partial board.

Only the first move on each copy is a hypothesis. Every later fill is an ordinary forced move. These are two different roof cells, not an exhaustive test of one cell’s candidates.

The shared conclusion
  • Remove 6 at r8c2: it sees both roofs.

Keep 6 in both roof cells. The native overlays show each trial’s exclusion of the same target notes; no warning or completed-solution claim is needed.
